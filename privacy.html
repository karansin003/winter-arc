Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 01, 2026

At Winter Arc 2026: Ghost Mode (accessible from https://arc-challenge.web.app), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Winter Arc and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, log in, or track habits on Winter Arc, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, username or alias, and target goals.
  • Habit & Progress Data: Daily habit completion status, journal reflections, rules, and streaks.
  • Log and Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, referring pages, and interaction timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain your personal Winter Arc tracker.
  • Sync your progress securely across your devices using Google Firebase.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our application features.
  • Send critical account emails such as password reset instructions.
  • Prevent fraudulent activity, spam, and abuse.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Winter Arc uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, user session tokens, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Winter Arc, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that Winter Arc has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data not sell the personal data. (Winter Arc does not sell personal user data).

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the right to access, rectification, erasure, restrict processing, and data portability.

7. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. Winter Arc does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: support@arc-challenge.web.app or through our official website.
