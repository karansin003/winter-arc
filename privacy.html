LEGAL COMPLIANCE & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 01, 2026 · We treat your private discipline data with absolute confidentiality.

At Winter Arc 2026: Ghost Mode (accessible from https://arc-challenge.web.app), protecting the privacy and security of our visitors and participants is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy document details the categories of information collected, processed, and safeguarded by Winter Arc and explains your individual rights under global data privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect

When you utilize our interactive tracker, register an account, or navigate our platform, we collect minimal data necessary to deliver your personalized experience:

  • Account Authentication Credentials: Your email address and encrypted password hash (managed directly via Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile & Ghost Alias: Your optional display name or ghost alias, target goal descriptions, daily focus targets, and custom personal mantras.
  • Habit Execution & Journal Data: Daily habit completion checkboxes across the 92-day grid, custom rules added or edited, and private journal reflection entries stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Technical Log Data: Like most web applications, our servers automatically register IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, referring URLs, and request timestamps to prevent unauthorized access and bot abuse.

2. How We Utilize Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain your 92-day habit tracker and calculation engines (streak flames, arc percentages).
  • To synchronize your habit history securely across your desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
  • To dispatch essential transactional emails, including self-service password recovery verification links.
  • To monitor server health, detect security vulnerabilities, and eliminate fraudulent spam activity.
  • We never sell, broker, monetize, or rent your personal habit logs, journal reflections, or contact information to any third party.

3. Cookies, Web Storage & Tracking Technologies

Winter Arc employs standard client-side technologies:

  • Essential Session Storage: Web tokens stored in localStorage or session cookies to keep you securely signed in and prevent data loss during network interruptions.
  • Preference Cookies: Remembering your privacy consent choices and active dashboard tab views.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies & Programmatic Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our website. Google utilizes cookies—specifically the DoubleClick DART cookie—to serve advertisements to visitors based upon their visit to this website and other destinations across the web.

You may opt out of personalized DART cookie advertising at any time by visiting Google's official Ad Privacy Settings page: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party ad networks may also deploy JavaScript, cookies, or Web Beacons within their respective sponsored units. They automatically receive your IP address when these units load. Winter Arc has no operational access to or control over cookies placed by independent advertisers.

5. CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to:

  • The right to know what personal data is collected, disclosed, or retained.
  • The right to request immediate deletion of personal information held by us.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (Winter Arc does not sell personal user information).
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Economic Area)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess comprehensive rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access: You may request copies of your personal data stored on our servers.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate or incomplete profile information directly in your profile settings or by contacting us.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You may request that we purge your entire account, habit history, and journal archives.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You have the right to restrict or object to the processing of your personal data under certain conditions.

7. Children's Privacy Notice

Winter Arc does not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our site, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such records from our databases.

8. Data Retention and Security Architecture

All data communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. Data stored within Google Cloud Firestore is protected by strict declarative security rules ensuring that only authenticated owners can read or write to their dedicated document path.

9. Privacy Contact Desk

For any privacy-related inquiries, data export requests, or erasure petitions, please reach out to our privacy officer at: support@arc-challenge.web.app or via our Contact Page.
