Pillar 01 · Body

Physical Conditioning

45–60 minutes of high-intensity physical training paired with an uncompromised baseline of 10,000 daily steps.

Pillar 02 · Fuel

Clean Nutrition & Hydration

Zero ultra-processed food or liquid sugar. High protein density and 3 to 4 liters of clean water every day.

Circadian Synchronization

In bed by 10:00 PM and awake by 5:00 AM. Establishing biological regularity and early morning momentum.

Information Diet

Read minimum 10 pages of high-yield non-fiction books daily while capping social media scrolling at 30 minutes.

Deep Work Mastery

2+ hours of distraction-free, uninterrupted deep focus dedicated to single-task study, engineering, or building.
