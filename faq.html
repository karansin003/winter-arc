Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the 92-day Winter Arc challenge, Ghost Mode discipline standards, data privacy, and tracker functionality.

What is the Winter Arc and when does it take place? +

The Winter Arc is a radical 92-day self-improvement period beginning October 1st and concluding December 31st. While mainstream culture often decelerates in the fourth quarter of the year, participants enter Ghost Mode to establish hardened discipline across physical fitness, nutrition, circadian health, reading, and deep work before January 1st arrives.

What does "Ghost Mode" mean? +

Ghost Mode refers to disappearing from unnecessary digital noise and vanity social media broadcasting to focus exclusively on quiet, high-leverage execution. It emphasizes keeping promises made to yourself in private rather than fishing for public validation.

What are the 5 core non-negotiable rules? +

The default baseline habits tracked each day are:

  • Physical: 45–60 minutes of intense physical training + 10,000 steps.
  • Nutrition: Zero ultra-processed junk food or liquid sugar + 3 to 4 liters of water.
  • Sleep: In bed by 10:00 PM and awake by 5:00 AM.
  • Mind: Read at least 10 pages of high-yield literature + maximum 30 minutes of social media.
  • Focus: 2+ hours of deep, uninterrupted, single-task work or study.
Can I customize or add my own rules in the tracker? +

Yes. In the My rules tab of the application, you can edit rule names, modify descriptions, delete rules that do not align with your physical requirements, or add new pillars (e.g. cold showers, language practice, meditation). Your custom rules automatically update your daily checklists across all 92 days.

How are the streak flame and percentage calculated? +

A day is deemed a Perfect Day when 100% of your active rules are checked off. Your current streak counts the consecutive unbroken string of perfect days up to the current date. Global percentage reflects the total completed promises across all 92 days of the arc.

What happens if I miss a day or habit? +

The cardinal rule of the Winter Arc is: never miss twice. If an emergency or illness prevents a perfect day, log your completed habits honestly. Your streak will reset, but your overall completion percentage and past grid entries remain saved. Regain your discipline the following morning at 5:00 AM.

Is my data private and encrypted? +

Yes. Winter Arc is engineered with a strict Private by Design philosophy. Your habits, streaks, and personal journal reflections are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore under your authenticated user ID. We never sell, rent, or publicly display your habit logs.

Can I install this on my iPhone or Android home screen? +

Yes! Winter Arc is designed as a standalone web application. On iOS Safari, tap the Share icon and select "Add to Home Screen". On Android Chrome, tap the three dots and select "Add to Home screen". It will open full-screen with no browser address bar.

Why is Winter Arc free to use? +

We believe foundational self-discipline tools should be accessible to everyone without paywalls or predatory subscriptions. The site is supported through ethical, non-intrusive Google AdSense advertising positioned away from interactive buttons.

Still have questions or suggestions?

Our team is here to support your 92-day journey. Reach out anytime with technical feedback or feature requests.
