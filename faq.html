Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the 92-day Winter Arc challenge: timeline dates, daily habit rules, tracker sync, streak mechanics, account privacy, and handling missed days.

1. Challenge Fundamentals & Dates

What is the Winter Arc Challenge? +

The Winter Arc Challenge is an intensive 92-day personal discipline and habit-building protocol running from October 1st to December 31st. Rather than slowing down during the fourth quarter and waiting for fragile January resolutions, participants enter Ghost Mode: eliminating digital noise, adhering to daily non-negotiable standards, and entering the new year at full momentum.

What are the exact dates and duration of the challenge? +

The challenge runs from October 1 at 12:00 AM through December 31 at 11:59 PM. This spans exactly 92 consecutive days, which represents 25.2% of the calendar year—an entire quarter of focused transformation.

Can I start if it is already past October 1st? +

Yes. While October 1 is the canonical starting date, the principle of the Winter Arc is self-discipline, not calendar perfection. You can register an account and start tracking today. Every day of discipline you bank before January 1st puts you miles ahead of waiting for the new year.

What does "Ghost Mode" mean? +

Ghost Mode means choosing private execution over public broadcasting. Instead of posting gym selfies or announcing intentions on social media for cheap dopamine, participants execute their daily standards quietly. The results speak for themselves when the arc concludes.

2. The 5 Core Pillars & Habit Customization

What are the 5 default non-negotiable pillars? +
  • Body (Physical): 45–60 minutes of training paired with 10,000 daily steps.
  • Fuel (Nutrition): Whole clean foods, zero ultra-processed junk/sugary drinks, and 3–4L water.
  • Sleep (Circadian): In bed by 10:00 PM and awake by 5:00 AM.
  • Mind (Information): 10+ pages of non-fiction reading and <30 minutes of social media.
  • Focus (Output): 2+ hours of uninterrupted deep work or study without distractions.
Can I customize the habits to match my schedule? +

Yes. In your dashboard under the "My Rules" tab, you can edit habit names and descriptions, remove rules, or add new custom habits (such as language study, meditation, or specific coding quotas). Learn more in our Habit Tracking Guide.

3. The Tracker, Sync & Mobile PWA

How does the Winter Arc Tracker save my progress? +

Whenever you check off a habit or write a journal entry, data is saved locally for instant responsiveness and synchronized to your private document in Google Cloud Firestore. When you switch devices or refresh, your full 92-day state is restored seamlessly.

How do I install the tracker as an app on my phone? +

The tracker is a Progressive Web App (PWA):

  • On iOS (Safari): Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen, scroll down, and select "Add to Home Screen".
  • On Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu in the upper right and select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

4. Accounts, Privacy & Data Security

Is my habit data and journal private? +

Yes, strictly private. We do not have public leaderboards or public profile feeds. Your habit completions and personal journal reflections are stored in Firestore under path rules that strictly restrict read and write access to your authenticated user ID. We never sell or broker user habit data.

How do I reset my password if I forget it? +

Visit the Account Recovery Page, enter your registered email address, and an official password reset link will be dispatched to your inbox within seconds.

Can I delete my account and data? +

Yes. You can contact support at karansin8672@gmail.com or use the Contact Form to request complete erasure of your account, habits, and journal archives under GDPR/CCPA guidelines.

5. Streaks, Missed Days & Motivation

What happens if I miss a day? Does my challenge fail? +

No! Missing one day resets your current streak flame, but does NOT erase your overall progress or the physiological benefits you have built. The cardinal law of the challenge is Never Miss Twice. Wake up tomorrow morning, win the first hour, and resume your streak immediately.

How do I stay consistent during the Thanksgiving / holiday season? +

Plan ahead: complete your workout, 10,000 steps, and reading early in the morning before family gatherings or festive dinners. Protect your sleep schedule as closely as possible, and remember that enjoying a planned meal with family does not mean abandoning your standards for the rest of the week.

6. Pricing & Advertising Standards

Is there any subscription or paid tier? +

No. The Winter Arc Tracker is completely free for all users. There are no premium paywalls or locked features.

Why are there advertisements on the site? +

We display non-intrusive programmatic advertisements via Google AdSense to cover server infrastructure and Firebase hosting costs while keeping the application 100% free for participants worldwide. We enforce strict ad policies: no deceptive placement, no popups, and no interference with daily tracking.
